# Lowrider + Low & Slow Kustoms (written, not yet tested in Studio)

An original lowrider (the **Royale Lowrider**, a long, low two-door hardtop; no real make or model) with working
air bags and hydraulics, and its own custom shop, **Low & Slow Kustoms**, where you buy the car and build it out.

## Files

| File | Goes in | What |
|---|---|---|
| `make_lowrider_vinyls.py` | (any Python + Pillow) | Draws the vinyl decal images into `lowrider_vinyls/` (run before the Blender script) |
| `build_lowrider.py` | (Blender) | Builds the car, the wheel and the shop. `blender --background --factory-startup --python build_lowrider.py` |
| `build_lowrider_parts.py` | (next to it) | Every swappable part: bumpers, grilles, hoods, spoilers, skirts, mirrors, seats, dashes, rims... (run by `build_lowrider.py`) |
| `Lowrider.glb` | import in Studio | Car pieces `Lowrider_*` (one mesh per material, every part variant included) + the wheel `LowriderWheel_*` with every rim, knock-off, tyre and whitewall |
| `LowriderVinyls.glb` | import in Studio | Every vinyl image on a copy of its panel, so Studio uploads the images for you |
| `lowrider_vinyls/*.png` | (source art) | The decal images (`<livery>_<panel>.png`); `sheet.png` shows them all over candy red |
| `LowSlowShop.glb` | import in Studio | The shop building `LowSlowShop_*` (~5k tris) |
| `NCLowriderData.lua` | ReplicatedStorage | Generated by the Blender script: wheel spots, hull boxes, seats, shop markers and colliders |
| `NCLowriderConfig.lua` | ReplicatedStorage | The tuning menu (`Config.Mods`: every option and price), paints, finishes, suspension, engines, driving numbers. Edit freely. |
| `NCLowriderKit.lua` | ReplicatedStorage | Builds the car / shop (mesh or part-made fallback) and paints a car from a look |
| `LowriderService.server.lua` | ServerScriptService | Shop, buying, saving (DataStore `NeonCoast_Garage_v1`), spawning, physics ownership, hop board |
| `LowriderClient.client.lua` | StarterPlayerScripts | Driving, hydraulics, wheels, HUD, phone buttons, hop meter, shop screen |

Needs `NCWallet` (already in ServerScriptService) so everything is paid for with the game's Cash.

## Install

1. Put the five scripts where the table says.
2. Put a part tagged `NC_LowriderShopSpot` where the shop should go (its front face points at the street). With no
   spot, the shop is built at `Config.ShopFallbackPosition` (260, ground, -260) and a warning says so.
3. Works right away with a part-made car and shop. For the Blender meshes: import `Lowrider.glb` and
   `LowSlowShop.glb`, then in the command bar:
   `require(game.ReplicatedStorage.NCLowriderKit).Prepare(workspace.Lowrider)` and the same for `workspace.LowSlowShop`.
   They land in `ReplicatedStorage.LowriderAssets` and every car / shop built after that uses them.
4. Vinyls: import `LowriderVinyls.glb` too (Studio uploads its images) and run `Prepare(workspace.LowriderVinyls)`.
   It saves each image's id in `ReplicatedStorage.LowriderAssets.Vinyls` and the shop's vinyls start working. If an
   image doesn't come through, upload that PNG from `lowrider_vinyls/` yourself and paste its id into
   `NCLowriderConfig.VinylIds` (e.g. `flames_side = "rbxassetid://..."`).
   (Same GLB upload folder issue as the buildings: see STATUS.md.)

## The car (Blender)

- Swept body lofted from cross sections, tubbed wheel wells, quad headlights, triple round taillights, side
  spears, rocker mouldings, door seams. Pillarless greenhouse with wide sail panels and chrome drip rails.
- Opening trunk lid ("Pop the trunk" prompt).
- Paint: the body's skin is split into UV-mapped paint panels (both sides, hood, trunk lid, roof). Vinyls are
  decal images printed straight onto those panels (MeshPart `TextureID`), flush in the paint, not raised pieces.
  Where an image is clear, the panel's candy colour shows through, so fades and lace blend into any paint.
- 15 liveries (all original, clean designs): pinstripe scrolls, twin stripes, checker bands, pearl scallops,
  sunset fade, retro waves, lightning, hot flames, ice flames, rose vines, tropical palms, patterns + lace,
  purple lace, a sunset mural on the trunk, and a show car set. Add more by drawing `<livery>_<panel>.png` and
  listing it in `Config.Art`.

## Driving and hydraulics

Raycast suspension on the driver's client: a spring + damper per corner and tyre grip / drive at each contact.
The hydraulics move each corner's ride height.

| Key (controller) | Does |
|---|---|
| W/S, A/D (triggers, stick) | drive, steer |
| Arrows (D-pad) | lift front / back / left / right; two at once lifts one corner |
| SPACE hold + let go (A) | front hop; let go just as the front lands to go higher |
| B (X) | body hop, all four |
| G (Y) | three-wheel: the front corner on the side you're steering lifts, and you can drive on three |
| X (L3) | ride height: ride, laid frame, lifted |
| V (R3) | dance (competition setup only) |
| F (B) | get out |

Phones get on-screen buttons. Kits (all in the config):

- **Stock springs**: just drives.
- **Air bags**: smooth, slow lifts and lays frame; no hopping.
- **Hydraulics 2-pump**: fast corners, small hop. **3-pump**: bigger hop + three-wheel.
  **4-pump competition hopper**: highest hop + dance.

Other players see the car move (physics replicates) and the wheels follow the driver's corner heights.

## The shop

Low & Slow Kustoms: two bays under a neon sign. Left bay has the paint turntable (customize here: the camera
circles your car while you shop and every choice previews live before you pay). Right bay is the hop pit: a
height board on the wall, your hop height pops up in inches, and the top five hoppers show on the board.

The tuning menu (GTA-style, left side): pick a category (Paint, Vinyls, Body kit, Wheels, Interior, Lights + glass,
Hydraulics, Extras), then a mod, then an option. The camera swings to that part, and pointing at an option previews
it on your car before you pick it. The cart at the bottom counts your changes and the total; BUY BUILD pays for all
of it at once, UNDO ALL goes back to what you own. Colours take a custom colour picker (hue + shade + hex) too.
MY BUILDS keeps 3 saved designs (saved to the DataStore; loading one puts it in the cart), and there's a
"Surprise me" random build and "Back to stock" to try ideas fast.

Around 60 mods with 300+ options: 22 paints + custom, 7 finishes (incl. chrome wrap, matte), two-tone roof, 15
liveries, every part above, finishes for each group of chrome (chrome, 24k gold, black chrome, rose gold, copper,
satin black, colour-matched), seat material (vinyl, leather, velour, suede, fabric) and colour, piping, carpet,
dash colour, 5 speedometer styles, wood trim, headlight and taillight colours, window tint, neon colour, 5
suspension kits and 4 engine tunes (more speed and acceleration). Anything you've paid for stays unlocked, so
switching back is free. Saves from the first version carry over (old wheels / whitewalls / extras map to the new
options).
